What was compared

The task was to retrieve five synthetic communication and identity facts, identify their sources and uncertainty, and distinguish superseded history from current information. The same authoritative topics were paired with either a detailed index or a compact routing index. The model was instructed to read whole records or navigate headings and sections.

CDetailedHeading map, selected sections, expansion when needed

The later retrieval panel crossed these four strategies with three index-size workloads (8, 40 and 64 KiB) and one 64 KiB topic workload: 16 answers, one per cell. Size labels describe the workload, not total returned bytes or a recommended threshold. Repeated reads, heading maps and fallback reads are charged.

The October 19 pilot is a separate four-answer experiment. Its assisted response schema and scoring history differ, so its results are not pooled with the later panel.

What this supports, and what it does not

The descriptive panel supports reporting these observed attempts and investigating their failure modes. It provides no unseen holdout, repeated samples per cell, confidence intervals, verified served-model identity or enforced isolation. Requested runtime was Codex CLI 0.163.0, GPT-6 Luna High; the served model remains unverified. Full Access left controller files accessible to the shell under procedural restrictions.

Prompt and scorer corrections affect interpretation

The frozen prompt requested controller-only fact IDs and protected-inventory IDs. The corrected contract matches candidate-visible authority and heading anchors instead. The first three 8 KiB answers were rescored offline after the scorer defect was found; all still failed. Original prompts, answers and traces were preserved. The later prompt correction cannot repair what those original runs were asked to do. The mismatch examples above also show why scorer failures deserve examination before being treated as broad capability failures.

Policy and provider claims remain separate

No strategy or threshold was selected by this benchmark. The later 180-day routine-audit heuristic was a separate correction, not a measured optimum. The user-approved closeout removed strict holdout/threshold selection and fresh activated backup publication/restore runs as closure gates. Offline backup controls and prior user-attested provider testing cover their recorded scope only; new exclusion-aware activated publication and recovery remain unmeasured.

The comparison JSON’s “production policy unchanged” disposition describes the benchmark checkpoint. It does not undo the subsequent audit-policy change. The earlier save-candidate identity and 31-case results likewise remain historical evidence for their frozen instructions.
