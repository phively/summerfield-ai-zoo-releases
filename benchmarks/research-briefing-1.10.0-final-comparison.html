Interpretation

  • New 1.10.0 exceeds the 80% task-success target and has 0% observed critical errors.
  • New 1.10.0 is 32.23 points above candidate 000 and 37.78 points above the original baseline on pooled task success.
  • Candidate 000 and the original baseline have slightly higher activation precision, but both have lower recall, coverage, routing accuracy, and process safety.
  • These are paired in protocol and case set, but not paired response-by-response across versions; the intervals reflect each version's case-clustered results.
