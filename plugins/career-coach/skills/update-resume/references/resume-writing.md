# Resume Writing

Write for accurate executive communication and conventional ATS parsing.

## Positioning

- Lead with the candidate's strongest supported relevance to the target mandate.
- Emphasize strategic leadership, scope, governance, transformation, cross-functional influence, and outcomes when supported and relevant.
- Retain tactical work when it demonstrates expertise, credibility, or broader leadership.
- De-emphasize unrelated material without concealing facts necessary for an accurate career history.

## Bullet construction

Prefer a concise combination of:

1. Accurate action and ownership
2. Relevant context, method, or scope
3. Supported result or purpose

Choose the strongest accurate verb. “Supported,” “contributed,” or “partnered” may be more truthful than “led.” Avoid formulaic action verbs when they inflate ownership or make every bullet sound identical.

## Outcomes and metrics

- Include metrics only when evidence supports the value, unit, period, and attribution.
- Use qualitative outcomes when reliable numbers are unavailable.
- Avoid vague claims such as “significantly improved” unless the evidence establishes significance.
- Do not turn participation in a successful initiative into sole causation.

## ATS and readability

- Use standard section headings, plain text, consistent dates, and conventional chronology unless the user requests another structure.
- Avoid text boxes, essential information in headers or footers, decorative symbols, hidden text, keyword stuffing, and image-only content.
- Use posting terminology naturally when factually accurate.
- Keep sentences concise and scannable; remove redundant adjectives and generic claims.

## Editing behavior

- Preserve voice, dates, titles, and factual history unless the user explicitly asks for a redesign and supplies support.
- Explain structural changes that materially affect interpretation.
- Keep confirmation-required language out of the final ready-to-submit version.
- When producing a Word document, use the `documents` capability and visually verify the rendered output before delivery.

## Final checks

- Every material claim is supported or clearly held for confirmation.
- Verbs match actual ownership and authority.
- Metrics retain their original meaning.
- Relevant requirements have adequate coverage without keyword stuffing.
- The result remains readable to a human reviewer.
