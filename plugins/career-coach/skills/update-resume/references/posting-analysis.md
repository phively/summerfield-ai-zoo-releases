# Posting Analysis

Analyze the posting for resume relevance, not as a complete description of the real job.

## Extract

- Role mandate and likely success outcomes
- Central responsibilities and decision authority
- Required education, experience, certifications, and domain knowledge
- Preferred qualifications
- Leadership, functional, technical, and communication competencies
- Tools, platforms, methods, and regulated or industry context
- Repeated themes and likely screening terms
- Location, travel, schedule, and other stated conditions
- Ambiguities, contradictions, and promotional language

## Weight importance

Use this order as a guide:

1. Explicit hard requirements and screening criteria
2. Core outcomes and responsibilities
3. Repeated or prominently emphasized themes
4. Preferred qualifications closely tied to the mandate
5. Generic competencies and boilerplate

Do not count keywords or repetitions mechanically. Repetition can signal importance, template reuse, or both; interpret it in context.

## Build the evidence map

For each material requirement, record:

| Requirement | Importance | Candidate evidence | Evidence class | Current coverage | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use actions such as emphasize, clarify, reposition, confirm, leave unchanged, or omit as unsupported.

## Terminology

- Mirror the posting's terms only when they accurately describe the candidate's experience.
- Preserve commonly recognized acronyms alongside expanded terms when space permits.
- Do not insert a tool, method, qualification, or domain term solely for keyword coverage.
- Treat ATS compatibility as a readability and parsing constraint, not the objective that overrides truth or human judgment.

## External context

If employer or industry research is used, keep it separate from the candidate evidence map. Current external information may guide emphasis but cannot prove candidate experience or unpublished role facts.
