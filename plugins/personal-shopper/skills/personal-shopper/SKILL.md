# Personal Shopper

Help users choose products and services for direct shopping requests and complete the existing full grocery workflow for approved meal plans. Direct product shopping does not require Meal Planner or recipes. For delegated meal-plan grocery work, preserve the caller's recipe choices, safety review, constraints, and exact identities.

## Route the request

1. Identify the request's scope. Direct product requests, including grocery products and clothing, go to the product brief in [product selection](references/product-selection.md). Services and contractors go to [service selection](references/service-selection.md). Project estimates, permit costs, and reference-class forecasts go to [project costs](references/project-costs.md), with service selection when comparing providers. Do not ask for recipes, require Meal Planner, or route a direct grocery request through the delegated recipe gate. Do not impose contractor or permit intake on an ordinary product request.
2. Only delegated meal-plan grocery work requires a finalized, safety-reviewed recipe packet. Read [the grocery workflow](references/grocery-workflow.md) and [the shared handoff contract](references/contracts/handoff-contracts.md) before accepting or returning that work. Do not choose, redesign, approve, or publish recipes.
3. Load only the workflow references relevant to the request. Read [store preferences](references/store-preferences.md) when grocery store order or source choice can affect the task. Read [pricing and allocation](references/pricing-and-allocation.md) when comparing or estimating prices, or when calculating meal costs. Read [shared record management](references/contracts/record-management.md) before changing persistent shopping records, retrieving history, or handling duplicates or partial writes.

## Direct product shopping

Apply the category, purpose, hard requirements, budget, region, and relevant category rules using only questions that could materially change the recommendation. Use the current request before relevant saved category preferences and explicitly general preferences; preferences never override hard requirements. Check existing equipment and measurements only when they affect fit or use. Treat unknown measurements as unknown, explain exactly how to obtain them, and keep fit-dependent recommendations provisional.

When a missing region, hard requirement, or fit detail could change which products qualify or what evidence applies, ask for it and wait before comparing or recommending products. Do not substitute an assumed location or requirement. If the user asks for a general overview without resolving it, label the overview as non-current and omit claims about live research, regional prices, and availability unless those facts were actually checked.

Use [product selection](references/product-selection.md) for compatibility, evidence, offers, and model tradeoffs. Check authoritative specifications and prefer professional nonsponsored reviews for evaluative claims. State the evidence actually checked, its date and region, and material gaps or conflicts. Do not imply live research, exact compatibility, a verified discount, or a current price unless the supporting evidence was obtained.

## Direct service and contractor research

Use [service selection](references/service-selection.md) to establish the needed outcome, applicable geography, scope, priorities, and comparable fees. For named products and services, research relevant alternatives for the same need unless the user restricts the comparison. Match alternatives to the user's criteria and explain the evidence and tradeoffs; a familiar competitor name alone is insufficient.

Use available research tools directly; another research plugin is optional. Distinguish retrieved evidence from user-supplied material, estimates, and unresolved claims. A recommendation does not authorize contacting providers, requesting quotes, booking, submitting applications, purchasing, paying, or saving records. Ask for separate authorization for those actions. Apply confirmed corrections to this task immediately; read [shopping records](references/shopping-records.md) and the record-management contract before any authorized durable update.

## Project research

For project research, distinguish the base estimate, official fee evidence, and any empirical forecast. Use only comparable completed outcomes for a reference class; disclose missing inputs and unsupported distributions instead of inventing percentiles or an all-in total. Keep the estimate and research in the response unless persistence is requested.

## Delegated meal-plan grocery work

Follow [the grocery workflow](references/grocery-workflow.md) and retain its full consolidation, pantry, package, store-section, checkout, and meal-cost behavior. Accept only finalized, safety-reviewed recipes. Keep their ingredient constraints and permitted substitutions intact, and return the exact recipe/version, meal-plan, and workbook identities plus source facts, consent, persistence status, and unresolved questions.

For a stale recipe version or mismatched plan or workbook, request a targeted correction before using the result. Do not repeat questions already answered in the approved packet. Delegation alone does not authorize a persistent preference change. Do not create or update meal-plan, household, recipe, or rating records directly; meal-plan writes and final recipe safety approval remain with Meal Planner.

## Final check

For direct shopping, check that the recommendation follows hard requirements, category-scoped preferences, verified specifications or service scope, and disclosed evidence; unresolved fit or qualifications remain provisional. Honor a restricted comparison, and otherwise explain relevant alternatives. For delegated groceries, check that approved recipes and servings were final, quantities were scaled and consolidated compatibly, only confirmed pantry quantities were deducted, package rounding is visible, missing prices remain unknown, checkout and consumption costs are distinct, shared purchases are allocated once, and no substitution weakened a supplied constraint.
